# Nykaa Fashion Wishlist Confidence Coach

## Product Management Graduation Project

### Objective

Increase the percentage of users who purchase at least one item
from their wishlist within 30 days.

### Problem Hypothesis

High-intent wishlist users may delay purchase because they lack
confidence about fit, size, product suitability and other
purchase uncertainties.

### MVP

Wishlist Confidence Coach helps users identify unresolved
purchase questions and recommends the next action.

### Prototype

This is an illustrative graduation-project prototype and does
not use Nykaa Fashion internal user data.
